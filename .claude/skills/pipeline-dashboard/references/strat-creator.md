# strat-creator

The strat-creator pipeline auto-generates and refines strategic initiative tickets in RHAISTRAT. It creates initiatives from various inputs, refines descriptions, validates against a quality rubric, tags release candidates, and flags items needing human review. Source: `jwforres/rfe-creator` (strat-creator skills are packaged in the same plugin). Labels follow `strat-creator-*` and `strat-epics-*` patterns.

**Projects with labels today**: RHAISTRAT
