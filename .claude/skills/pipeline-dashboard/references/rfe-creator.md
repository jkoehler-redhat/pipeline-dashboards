# rfe-creator

The rfe-creator pipeline auto-generates and refines RFE (Request for Enhancement) tickets from customer feedback and internal sources. It creates issues, revises them for quality, splits compound RFEs, assesses feasibility, and flags items needing human attention. Source: `jwforres/rfe-creator` (found via `opendatahub-io/skills-registry`). Label definitions in `scripts/submit.py` (`FEASIBILITY_LABELS`) and `.claude/skills/rfe.submit/SKILL.md` (Labeling Scheme table).

**Projects with labels today**: RHAISTRAT, RHAIRFE, RHOAIENG
